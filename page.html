1 · SHENZHEN · THE DECLARATION

THE PEACE DECLARATION — AND THE BACKLASH

The full text of the Trump–Xi–Putin “peace declaration” is published on Friday: it pledges UN reform, more refined-petroleum supply, state consent and immunity of state officials, and a nuclear-risk dialogue, reaffirming “a nuclear war cannot be won and must never be fought”. It says nothing about Ukraine or Iran and sets no arms-control talks. Trump calls the summit “bigger than Yalta”; Germany’s Merz says “a Yalta format will not bring peace”, France’s Macron says it “does not lead to a reorganisation of the world”, and Senate Democrat leader Schumer calls it “a historic embarrassment”. [Reuters]

2 · EUROPE · UKRAINE WAR

RUSSIA HITS KYIV IN OVERNIGHT BLITZ

Russia launches a mass missile attack overnight into Friday, hours after Putin vowed retaliation for the Crimean Bridge strike; Tu-160 bombers, ballistic missiles and drones target Kyiv, Sumy, Poltava, Lviv and Kryvyi Rih. Explosions begin around 01:25, with fires in Obolonskyi, Podilskyi and Holosiivskyi districts. No casualties initially reported.

[Reuters]

3 · ASIA-PACIFIC · WEATHER

CYCLONE AKVAN DUE ASHORE

Akvan is due ashore Friday near Gopalpur/Haripur, Odisha, with winds of 90–100 kmph, gusting to 110. More than 300,000 evacuated; 423 rescue teams, 2,523 relief camps and 69 train cancellations mark the preparations. It is the third severe cyclone in under a month, after Arnab and Murasu.

[IMD]

4 · WORLD · CLIMATE

COP31 CLOSES — WITHOUT THE FOSSIL WORD

COP31 closes Friday in Antalya as negotiators race the deadline. Thursday’s draft cut the cover decision from 144 paragraphs to 80 and dropped every fossil-fuel reference, while retaining a 35%-by-2035 electrification goal. The $120bn-a-year adaptation-finance tripling remains contested.

[Reuters]
